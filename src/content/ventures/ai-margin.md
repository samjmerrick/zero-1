## The idea

AI features have a running cost on every request, which makes pricing hard. AI Margin would model cost per user across model choice, prompt size, caching and pricing tier, and later watch real usage for slipping margins.

## Where it is

Exploring. The first test is a free calculator published alongside an essay on pricing AI products.
