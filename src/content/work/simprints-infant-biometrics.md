## The problem

Health workers need to know which infant they’re treating, but there was no reliable way to recognise infants without contact, offline, on the cheap phones used in the field.

## What I did

I lead a $6m initiative at Simprints to build the first reliable contactless infant biometric recognition algorithm, working with a team of ML engineers, a backend engineer and a data scientist. The study followed 13,000 infants monthly for 12 months in Ghana and Bangladesh, with more than 4 million images.

## Result

The true match rate at a 1% false match rate rose from around 40% to around 90% over a three-month growth period. The models run fully offline on low-spec Android phones, using only the phone camera.
