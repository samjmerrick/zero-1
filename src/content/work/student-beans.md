## The problem

Student Beans runs a B2B2C student loyalty platform. Its website was slow, and slow pages cost conversions.

## What I did

I led the growth tribe from 2021 to 2022, including a rebuild of the website.

## Result

Page-load times fell roughly threefold, conversion rose by around 50%, and organic traffic grew by around 350%.
