[HandDeco](https://www.handdeco.com/) is a creative studio in the Zürich Weinland that works where colour, textiles and art meet. It sells yarn in hundreds of colours from brands such as DMC, Rowan and Rico, plus stitch kits, patterns and in-person colour consultations.

Its most distinctive product is a made-to-order service that turns a customer’s photo into a cross-stitch or tapestry pattern, with all the materials delivered on request.

## What Zero-1 built

The entire site. It runs on Shopify, sells in German to customers across Europe and beyond, and takes payment in local currencies through Shop Pay, PayPal, Klarna and Apple Pay.
