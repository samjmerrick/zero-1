## The problem

The Virgin Group wanted one loyalty programme across its brands, to succeed Virgin Atlantic Flying Club and its roughly 2 million members.

## What I did

I was a product manager on Virgin Red’s founding team from 2018 to 2021, and introduced an ML-powered member-intelligence platform for reward targeting.

## Result

The reward targeting was linked to a 30% rise in engagement.
