## The problem

Sixteenth is an influencer management agency. Its creators needed a steady stream of content ideas that fitted their voice and their brand deals.

## What I did

As the first in-house technical hire, reporting to the founder, I led Spark Jam: an LLM-powered tool that generates content ideas for creators. I managed a technology budget of around $100k.

## Why it matters here

It’s the same work Zero-1 does: take a fuzzy idea, find where AI earns its place, and ship something people use.
