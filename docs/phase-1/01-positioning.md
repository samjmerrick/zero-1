## Model: founder-led

The brand is a person, not an agency. Copy is written in the first person ("I"), with "we" reserved for the moment a client joins the work ("we'll test this with your users"). Every page should make it obvious who you're hiring.

- Logo lockup: `Zero-1` wordmark, with "by Sam Merrick" as the descriptor in nav/footer.
- Photo of Sam on Home and About. No stock imagery.
- samjmerrick.com stays the personal CV/research site; zero-1.studio is the commercial front door. Each links to the other.

## The problem we sell against

AI has made prototypes cheap and businesses no easier. Most AI ideas die for one of four reasons:

1. **It's a wrapper.** No data, workflow or distribution advantage, so the next model release eats it.
2. **The unit economics don't work.** Inference cost per user is higher than anyone will pay.
3. **It works on the demo, not in the field.** Accuracy collapses on real users, real devices, real data.
4. **Nobody tested the riskiest assumption first.** Months spent building the easy part.

Zero-1 exists to kill bad ideas cheaply and ship good ones properly.

## Audiences (in priority order)

1. **Founders & operators with domain expertise** — they know an industry problem, want an AI product, and need a technical product partner. *Entry offer: Ideation Sprint.*
2. **Established companies exploring AI ventures** — innovation leads, heads of product, CEOs of mid-size firms who want a new AI revenue line validated before committing a team. *Entry offer: Validation & Prototype.*
3. **Mission-driven orgs deploying AI in hard conditions** — NGOs, health, humanitarian: offline, low-cost devices, sensitive data. *Entry offer: Fractional AI Product Lead.* (Smallest volume, strongest differentiation.)

## Voice

- **Plain and direct.** Short declarative sentences. Same voice as samjmerrick.com.
- **Show the number.** Prefer "~40% → ~90%" over "dramatically improved".
- **Sceptical of hype.** We're the people who ask "what does it cost per request?" Never say "revolutionary", "unlock", "harness the power of", "cutting-edge".
- **Warm, not cute.** Drop the 🚀 from the current site.

Words we use: *ship, test, field, real users, unit economics, riskiest assumption, go/no-go, evaluation.*
